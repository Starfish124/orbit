# 6 · Don't merge across words

**The idea.** Train on real text and BPE happily learns tokens like
`"e t"` or `"dog."`: pieces of two words glued. That wastes vocabulary.
GPT-2's fix: first **split** the text into chunks with a regex, train and
encode each chunk on its own, so no merge can reach across a chunk edge.

`re.findall(pattern, text)` returns every piece that matches, left to right.
Our simplified GPT-2 pattern (stdlib `re`, ASCII letters only). Put it at
the top of `bpe.py` exactly as written:

```python
SPLIT = r"'(?:s|t|re|ve|m|ll|d)| ?[A-Za-z]+| ?[0-9]+| ?[^\sA-Za-z0-9]+|\s+(?!\S)|\s+"
```

Read it as "or"-ed options: an ending like `'s`; a word with at most one
space **in front**; a number; a run of other symbols; whitespace. So
`"hello world"` becomes `["hello", " world"]`: a space can only ever start
a token. Accents and emoji fall under "other symbols"; nothing is lost.

```python
import re
print(re.findall(r"[0-9]+|[a-z]+", "abc123de4"))   # ['abc', '123', 'de', '4']
```
