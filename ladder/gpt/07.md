# 7 · Attention: every position asks the past a question

**The idea.** A bigram sees one letter back. *Self-attention* lets each
position look at **all earlier positions** and decide how much each
matters. Every position's vector `x` goes through three `Linear` layers:

- `q = query(x)`: what am I looking for?
- `k = key(x)`: what do I contain?
- `v = value(x)`: what do I hand over if picked?

Position `t` scores each position `s ≤ t`: `dot(q[t], k[s]) / sqrt(n_embd)`
(dividing keeps scores from growing with the size). Softmax the scores into
weights; the output is the weighted sum of the `v` vectors.

*Causal* means `t` never looks at `s > t`: when predicting the next letter,
the future is the answer, so peeking is cheating. Just loop `s` over
`range(t + 1)`.

A weighted average, plain floats, other topic: a grade.

```python
scores  = [7.0, 9.0, 6.0]
weights = [0.2, 0.5, 0.3]            # sum to 1
print(sum(w * s for w, s in zip(weights, scores)))    # 7.7
```

The *dot product* of two vectors: `sum(a * b for a, b in zip(u, w))`.
